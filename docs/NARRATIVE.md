# Narrative reference

## Northgate

A residential tower operates under a late-night access protocol after a series of identity incidents. The player is a temporary control-desk operator; official records are incomplete and not always internally consistent.

## F.A.F.E.

F.A.F.E. is the response unit dispatched through the desk phone's 998 line. It can secure an incident, but false calls reduce confidence in the operator.

## The Patient

The Class-X subject is unusually calm and appears to mirror familiar expressions. It speaks in short, ambiguous sentences and uses the interrogation to question how much the operator remembers.

## Tone

Keep dialogue restrained, specific, and unsettling. Prefer a small contradiction over a long exposition paragraph. All characters and organizations in this prototype are fictional.
