# Controls

## Day shift

- **Allow**: admit the visitor.
- **Deny**: keep the visitor outside.
- **Call 998**: dispatch F.A.F.E. The result arrives after a short delay.
- **Question buttons**: ask about the visitor's relative, neighbor, or usual phrase.
- **Hold right mouse button on the ID**: magnify its details.
- **Evidence board**: review clues collected during the shift.

## Night drive

- **Drive 10 min**: advance time, consume fuel, and risk tire damage.
- **Check radio**: inspect a suspicious transmission.
- **Use shotgun**: consume the single available shell to interrupt a possible encounter.
- **Arrive at HQ**: continue to the Class-X interrogation.

## Saving

Progress is stored locally in the current browser. The prototype does not synchronize saves between devices.
