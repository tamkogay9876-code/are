# Game design: Are You Lying?

## High concept

The player works the Northgate Residential Control desk. Most visitors are ordinary residents, but some are impostors copying a real person's identity. The job is to compare an ID with several independent records before deciding who may enter.

At midnight, the game shifts from quiet verification to a dangerous drive to police headquarters, followed by an interrogation of a Class-X suspect.

## Core loop

1. Read the visitor's story and the displayed ID.
2. Compare room, ID number, microcode, relative, and neighbor details.
3. Ask a question and record the answer as evidence.
4. Allow the resident, deny entry, or call 998 for F.A.F.E.
5. Review the consequences and continue until the shift ends.
6. Drive to headquarters and question the Class-X subject.

## Design goals

- Reward careful cross-checking rather than random guessing.
- Make each visitor a small deduction puzzle.
- Let mistakes change trust, reputation, and suspicion.
- Use short text, environmental sound, and a restrained interface to build unease.
- Keep the prototype easy to extend with content packs and localization.
